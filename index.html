Settings

VR (Quest / PCVR): Left stick move (click = sprint) · Right stick snap-turn, push forward = teleport · Grip = grab / open crates / revive · Trigger = fire / use medical · A/X = drop mag or pump · Grab a mag from your belt-front with the off hand, push it into the gun · Off-hand on the foregrip = two-hand aim (pump the shotgun by sliding it) · Store items at hips, back and chest · Y = flashlight, hold Y = menu. Hand tracking: fist = grab, pinch = trigger, pinch empty left hand = walk.
Desktop: Click to look · WASD · Shift sprint · C crouch · E pick up / open / revive (hold) · LMB fire/use · R reload · Q drop · 1–6 inventory · F flashlight · V mute.
Find the EXIT to go deeper. Gunshots, talking and crates make noise.
