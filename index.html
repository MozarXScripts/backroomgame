Sound pack (use real recordings)

Pick audio files (ogg/mp3/wav). The file name picks what it replaces — e.g. yell.ogg, yell_2.mp3, pistol.wav. Slots: pistol, smg, shotgun, rifle, yell (entity spots you), growl, creak (Stilt), death, attack, monsterstep, step, hurt, magin, magout, rack, pump, crate, whisper, distant (far-off scares), breath, flesh, ambience (looping room tone). Several files per slot are picked at random. They're saved on this device. Free CC0 sounds: freesound.org (filter by CC0) and opengameart.org.

Using built-in sounds.

VR (Quest / PCVR): Left stick move (click = sprint) · Right stick snap-turn, push forward = teleport · Grip = grab / open crates / revive · Trigger = fire / use medical.
Manual reload (VR): A/X drops the mag (or grip the mag to pull it out) → off-hand grip at the ammo pouch on the front of your belt → push the mag into the magwell → grab the slide / charging handle and pull it back. Shotgun: take shells from the belt one at a time, push into the loading port under the gun, then slide the pump back & forward. Off-hand on the foregrip = two-hand aim (pistol too: wrap your off hand around your gun hand). Store items at hips, back and chest pouches. Y = flashlight, hold Y (or tap B with an empty right hand) = menu; tap Y, B, point the trigger away from the panel, or press ✕ CLOSE MENU to close it. Hand tracking: fist = grab, pinch = trigger, touch your left-wrist watch with your right finger for the menu. Make a fist and swing at people to punch.
Sandbox: TAB (desktop) / hold Y (VR) opens Spawn · Guns · Gunsmith · World. X fire mode, L laser/light (VR: B/Y and gun-hand stick click). Blades: LMB slash, RMB stab (VR: swing for real). E drags bodies (VR: grip a body). Empty hands punch: LMB with nothing held (desktop), or swing a fist (VR).
Desktop: Click to look · WASD · Shift sprint · C crouch · E pick up / open / revive (hold) · LMB fire/use · R mag out / mag in / load shell · RMB or T rack slide / pump · Q drop · 1–6 inventory · F flashlight · V mute. Sprint: Shift (desktop) / click the left stick or push it all the way forward (VR).
